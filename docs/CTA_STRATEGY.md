# CTA STRATEGY

> Canonical conversion and advisory CTA strategy.

---

# PRIMARY GOAL

Convert technical visitors into:

* consulting leads
* advisory opportunities
* DevRel partnerships
* technical collaborations
* startup architecture engagements

---

# CTA POSITIONING

CTAs should feel:

* strategic
* premium
* architectural
* technical

NOT:

* sales-heavy
* aggressive
* freelancer-style

---

# PRIMARY CTA TYPES

## Architecture Advisory

Example:
“Need help designing production-grade AI systems?”

---

## Engineering Audit

Example:
“Book a deep technical architecture review.”

---

## DevRel Partnership

Example:
“Looking for advanced technical content around AI infrastructure and orchestration?”

---

## AI Platform Strategy

Example:
“Design scalable AI-native systems with production reliability.”

---

# CTA LOCATIONS

Include CTAs:

* homepage hero
* after case studies
* after engineering articles
* services sections
* blog footers
* open-source showcases

---

# CTA PRINCIPLES

CTAs should:

* target technical buyers
* emphasize business impact
* emphasize scalability
* emphasize reliability
* reinforce authority

Avoid:

* generic “Contact Me”
* “Hire Me”
* low-ticket language

---

# PREFERRED LANGUAGE

Preferred:

* advisory
* architecture
* systems
* platform
* infrastructure
* orchestration
* reliability

Avoid:

* cheap freelancing language
* generic agency wording
